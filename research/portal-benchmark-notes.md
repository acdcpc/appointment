# Patient Portal and Appointment Benchmark Notes

## Sources reviewed

- Zocdoc’s patient self-scheduling guidance emphasizes service-specific online eligibility, provider rules and buffers, confirmation, and a staged rollout rather than exposing every visit type at once.
- Doctolib’s published product listing emphasizes booking, appointment reminders, and calendar support.
- MyChart’s published provider help and health-system descriptions emphasize a central place for appointments, care-team messages, results, and medication information.
- Practo’s published product descriptions emphasize appointments, health-record access, and clear health-management entry points.

## Safe adaptations for Rainbow Child Development Clinic

1. Keep service-aware booking and clinician schedule rules, but add a concise parent-facing pre-visit preparation panel for the next appointment.
2. Make the next appointment actionable with an explicit calendar export action and a clear follow-up booking route.
3. Give Dr. Ojha a compact daily operational focus view that groups today’s visits, future schedule changes, and guardian follow-up work without exposing information on the public screen.
4. Preserve the clinic’s review-first approach: communication, report sharing, and reminders must remain clinician-reviewed rather than auto-sent.
