Route-by-route UI audit

Rainbow Child Development Clinic — single-clinician pediatric practice of Associate Professor Dr. Anil Ojha. Findings recorded from the running Expo web build and the source, with the two previously open findings resolved by explicit decision rather than silently in code.

Date 2026-09-15 Scope Parent, clinician and governance routes Constraint No new project, no paid messaging, no authority changes

Open findings resolved

Both decisions are recorded here and mirrored in design.md; the audit is the record of why.

F1 · colour

Decision — adopt deep navy #092C4C as the single canonical primary. Teal #0E7490 is retained as the secondary healthcare token for links, selected and availability states and chart series; coral #F97360 is the booking action.

Rationale: navy is the later, deliberate rebrand and matches the redesign brief's “deep navy” direction. Nothing is removed — teal simply stops being treated as primary. Updated: theme.config.js, PWA theme-color, HTML shell meta, the WHO growth chart series, and the palette table in design.md.

F6 · phone OTP

Decision — documentation-only option, no mockup restoration. design.md now states plainly that no phone-or-OTP UI exists today, that parent sign-in is Supabase email + password, and that phone OTP is a documented future direction behind the lib/supabase-auth.ts seam. The completed-task claims in todo.md are marked superseded.

Rationale: restoring a mockup that authenticates nothing into a production sign-in screen adds parent confusion and no user value; the honest documentation route is smaller and safer, and matches the product decision already taken when the mockup was removed.

Cross-cutting findings

  • Devanagari line-height. Nepali secondary lines sat at a Latin 1.3 ratio; raised on parent routes, and the web build now loads Mukta and Noto Sans Devanagari ahead of the Latin face.
  • Contrast on warm fills. Coral and green fills previously carried white text; fill/text pairs are now ink-on-coral and white-on-navy, both ≥4.5:1.
  • Demo identity leakage. Home, Profile and Records could present fictional children as real; parent routes now distinguish linked child, account-not-linked and signed-out states.
  • Dark-mode tint leaks. Light-only tint literals were hardcoded in several screens; parent screens now read tint tokens so dark mode does not expose light surfaces.
